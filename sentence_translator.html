Sentence Translator

Train custom gesture → sentence mappings or test your trained model for real-time recognition.

📝
Step 1 — Add Your Sentences
Enter sentences you want to teach. Each needs a unique hand gesture.
Tip: Add 2–10 distinct sentences. Each sentence needs its own unique gesture. More samples per sentence = better accuracy.
🎥
Step 2 — Record Gesture Samples
A camera window will open. Show your gesture 8 times for each sentence.

For each sentence, a camera window opens. Press R to start recording your gesture, then press SPACE when done. Repeat 8 times. The system collects the hand landmark sequences as training data.

⚡
Step 3 — Train the Model
LSTM neural network trains on your collected samples. Takes 1–3 minutes.
