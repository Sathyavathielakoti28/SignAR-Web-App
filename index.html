📋 OVERVIEW

SignAR is an AI-powered ASL (American Sign Language) translator that uses your webcam and MediaPipe to detect hand gestures in real-time. It supports two modes: Letter Translation and Sentence Translation.

📁 REQUIRED FILES (place in app folder)

  • hand_landmarker.task — MediaPipe hand model
  • sign_language_translator.py — Letter mode backend
  • sentence_engine.py — Sentence training backend
  • sentence_translator.py — Sentence recognition backend

🔧 STEP 1 — INSTALLATION

1

Install Python 3.8+

Download from python.org and ensure it's added to your system PATH during installation.

2

Install required libraries

Open a terminal in the project folder and run:

pip install flask flask-cors opencv-python mediapipe numpy scikit-learn pyttsx3 torch

3

Start the web app

In the project folder, run: python app.py

Then open http://localhost:5000 in your browser.

🔤 STEP 2 — LETTER TRANSLATOR

1

Open Letter Translator from Dashboard

Click "Letter Translator" — a camera window will open on your desktop.

2

Show ASL hand signs

Hold each letter sign steady for ~1.5 seconds in front of the camera. Recognized letters appear on screen and build into a word/sentence.

3

Controls

C = Clear text  |  Q = Quit the window

💡 Tips: Good lighting is essential. Keep your hand centered in frame, about 1–2 feet from the camera. Plain backgrounds improve accuracy significantly.

💬 STEP 3 — SENTENCE TRANSLATOR (Train)

1

Add your sentences

Type the sentences you want to teach the system. Each sentence needs a unique gesture/sign you'll perform.

2

Record gesture samples

A camera window opens. Press R to start recording your gesture for the displayed sentence. Press Space when done. Repeat 8 times per sentence for best results.

3

Train the model

After collecting samples, click "Train Model". Training takes 1–3 minutes. Progress is shown in real-time.

🧪 STEP 4 — SENTENCE TRANSLATOR (Test)

1

Launch the recognizer

Click "Test / Recognize" from the Sentence Translator page. A live camera window opens.

2

Perform your gesture

Show the trained gesture to the camera. If recognized, the sentence is displayed and spoken aloud via text-to-speech!

3

Unknown gesture?

If the system doesn't recognize a gesture, it will prompt you to teach it by providing the sentence label for training.

🎯 For best accuracy: Collect at least 8 samples per sentence. Make each gesture clearly distinct from others. Consistent hand position and good lighting are key.
